## Notes

1. Material: EN AW-6082-T6 billet (alloy group 6xxx, Al-Mg-Si).
2. Yield strength Rp0.2 >= 250 MPa. SPECIAL CHARACTERISTIC SC-01, controlled per control plan CP-BR220-R3.
3. Fastener holes: 4 x diameter 8.5 mm H12, edges deburred, no cracks or tears visible at 10x magnification.
4. Finish: anodise type II, thickness 10 to 15 micrometres, black, per specification SPEC-FIN-12.
5. Material certificate EN 10204 type 3.1 required with each lot.
6. Break all sharp edges 0.3 mm max unless noted.
