## 1. Scope and completeness

Scope: series production parts released at Torino Plant 1 (TO1) and Torino Plant 2 (TO2) for customers Stellantis (STL) and Cantieri Lombardi Mobility S.p.A. (CLM).
This record is the complete list of approved material sources for the parts, plants and customers in scope.
A source that is not listed for a part, plant and customer has no approval.
Approval is granted per part, per receiving plant and per customer, never per supplier.
An approval covers the named manufacturing location only.
A supplier audit result does not grant a part approval.
Torino Plant 3 (TO3) is not in scope of this record.
Service and aftermarket parts are not in scope of this record.
Material certificates are not stored in this record.
