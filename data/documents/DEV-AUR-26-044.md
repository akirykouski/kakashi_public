Subject: Deviation DEV-AUR-26-044 - BR-220 blanks from Meccanica Chisone

Dear colleagues,

following your request of 10 November and our call this morning, Aurelia grants deviation DEV-AUR-26-044.

You may ship BR-220 brackets made from blanks supplied by Meccanica Chisone (SUP-C) to Aurelia, assembled at
your Beinasco plant, instead of blanks from Alluminio Bormida (SUP-B).

This deviation permits the change of source only. It does not cover any change of material, process or
coating. It is limited to 2 lots and expires on 31 December 2026, whichever comes first.

The deviation is valid from today. Every part of the 2 lots needs 100% inspection of the yield strength
certificate. A full PPAP resubmission is still required before Chisone becomes a permanent source.
