Aurelia Automobili - Supplier Quality Manual, revision 5, section 7 "Changes". Replaces revision 4.
7.1 The supplier shall obtain written approval from Aurelia (PPAP resubmission) before shipping parts
after any of: a change of source, a change of material, or a change of manufacturing process.
7.2 A change of coating or surface treatment requires notification to Aurelia only; approval is not
required. The notification shall reach Aurelia at least 60 days before implementation.
7.3 Approval is granted per part number and per manufacturing site. An approval for one part does not
extend to another part, even if made of the same material.
