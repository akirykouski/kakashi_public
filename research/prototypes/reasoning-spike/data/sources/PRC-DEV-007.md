# Procedure PRC-DEV-007: Temporary Deviation

## 1. Purpose

Defines when a temporary deviation may be requested from the customer if an approved source cannot deliver on time.

## 2. Limits

A temporary deviation may cover at most 2000 pieces and at most 6 weeks.
For parts with fastener holes, 100 percent inspection of the fastener holes is required for every piece shipped under the deviation.
A temporary deviation does not approve a new material source.

## 3. Decision

The Plant Quality Manager decides whether a deviation request is filed with the customer.
The allowed responses to a late approved source are: expedite the approved source, or request a temporary deviation within the limits of section 2.
