Illustrative excerpt written for a synthetic dataset. The wording is invented and is not Stellantis text. Each requirement is numbered by the real IATF 16949 clause it supplements.

## Scope

These requirements apply to every part delivered to Stellantis for a program listed in Annex A.
Annex A: programs STL-Torino-27 and STL-Torino-31.
Service parts are governed by a separate document, STL-CSR-SP-01, which is not part of this excerpt.

## Product and process changes

8.3.6.1 Any change to a requirement stated on a customer drawing, including material and finish, needs written approval from Stellantis before the change is implemented.
8.3.3.3 Any change to a characteristic designated special, to its control method or to its sampling frequency needs written approval from Stellantis before the change is implemented.
7.1.5.3.2 Verification of a special characteristic by an external laboratory needs prior notification to Stellantis and acceptance of the laboratory scope.

## Source changes

8.5.6.1 A change of material source, of a sub-supplier, or of the manufacturing location of a sub-supplier needs a documented risk analysis and written approval from Stellantis before any shipment of affected parts. This clause applies in addition to IATF 16949 8.5.6.1.
8.4.2.1 A change of subcontracted process, including surface treatment, needs notification to Stellantis and approval before shipment.

## Temporary deviations

8.7.1.1 A temporary deviation is requested through the supplier portal and needs approval before shipment.
