QP-15 Change-check policies, revision A. Synthetic document of the fictional company Valsangone Componenti, written for the hackathon.
1.1 An open 8D of a supplier counts against a change that brings a part to that supplier when the 8D names an alloy of the same alloy series as the part and the same failure feature as the part.
1.2 An open 8D that names the alloy of the part always counts, whatever the feature.
2.1 The approved supplier list of the Beinasco plant (PLT-BEI) is complete for series parts of Aurelia Automobili (CUST-AUR) made of 6xxx aluminium alloys: a supplier missing from the list for such a part is not approved.
2.2 For any other plant, customer, commodity, and for service parts, the approved supplier list is not declared complete: a missing approval is an open question for the supplier quality engineer.
3.1 A change of drawing material requires a material certificate (EN 10204 type 3.1) for the new material from the supplier who delivers it.
3.2 A material that is not in the material master is an open question for the quality engineer for aluminium parts.
3.3 A coating that is not in the coating catalogue is an open question for the process engineer.
3.4 A supplier that is not in the supplier master is an open question for the supplier quality engineer.
3.5 A part is made only in the material its drawing in force designates. A different material may not ship until the drawing is changed and the customer has approved it.
3.6 Missing strength data for a material is an open question for the quality engineer for aluminium parts.
