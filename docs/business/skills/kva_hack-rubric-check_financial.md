# KVA hack-rubric-check — what the jury scores for money (extract, from MCP get_hack_skill skills/hack-rubric-check/SKILL.md)

Gate 2, track 4-decision-models: Financial sustainability weight 17 (of 100; second only to Demo or reality 18).
Anchors (score 1..7):
- 1: No economic model in Control8
- 4: A model with a priced revenue line, volumes or acquisition missing
- 7: Revenue lines with price and volume, channels with cost per lead and conversion, a payback the team can defend at the financial validation
Rules: a [declared] fact can support a 4, not a 7. The jury card shows the economic model's headline (annual revenue, CAC payback), saved with update_economic_model; at 16:45 it closes.
Hand-in check: the economic model saved in Control8, with its headline. Price and volume in the model must equal the ones in the pitch artifact.
Gate 1 Willingness to invest reads the market the model rests on (7 = dense, paying market with a source: startups on the same problem, investors, buyers who already spend).
