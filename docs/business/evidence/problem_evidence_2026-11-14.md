# Problem Evidence (team research, 14 Nov 2026; pasted by Maksim from the team's "Problem Evidence" page)
Sources named in the page: docs/research/practitioner-voice.md and docs/research/online-confirmation.md (team repo).

- 26 practitioner quotes; 10 independent threads describe supplier/sub-supplier changes arriving late or not at all (mostly Elsmar Cove, 2000–2026). 6 sources: unclear whether customer notice/new PPAP needed. 5: change records in email/portals/spreadsheets. 4: rejected lots, sorting charged back ($30–50 an hour in one account), audit findings. 4: CSRs tracked by hand.
- Quotes: "cause, eng not notified of change (buried in email)" (Elsmar, 2010); "The assumption is that the customer will never find out..." (Elsmar, 2024); "sometimes it's easier to ask for forgiveness than permission" (Elsmar, 2023); counterpoint "We have not had any issue with suppliers making changes without notification." (Elsmar, 2023).
- Astemo Supplier Change Management Guidebook rev 4.0 (Dec 2025): 83% of significant supplier quality issues caused by failed change management (FY22–FY24); "silent change" incl. sub-supplier changes.
- Adient Global Supplier Standards Manual rev 10.0 (2023): written approval before every product/process change, ≥180 days ahead; otherwise New Business Hold, notice to IATF registrar, possible financial consequences.
- Ford recall 18V-806 (NHTSA): tier-3 changed moulding process; tier-2 not told.
- IATF top-10 major nonconformities Apr 2026 (VDA QMC): change control (8.5.6) NOT in the global top 10 → do not claim it is a top audit finding.
- Comparable software prices per year: mid-sized enterprise QMS $25,000–100,000 (Monetizely, Aug 2025); Arena median $55,970 (Vendr, 31 purchases); Greenlight Guru median $43,989 (Vendr); MasterControl median $115,673 (Vendr).
